PokeDex API

Welcome to the Pokédex 🔍 — your quick way to explore Pokémon data. Simply type a Pokémon’s name into the search bar and the app will instantly display its name along with a few key abilities.

Use this as a starting point to discover different Pokémon and understand how each one is unique. Each search gives you a quick snapshot, helping you recognize patterns in abilities and how different Pokémon compare. This is perfect for beginners who want to quickly learn without being overwhelmed by too much information.

As you continue using the app, try experimenting with different Pokémon names to see how the results change. Pay attention to how the API responds — this helps you understand how data is being fetched and displayed behind the scenes. If a Pokémon doesn’t appear, double-check your spelling to ensure accurate results.

This Pokédex is also a great way to practice interacting with real-world data in a simple interface. You can use it to compare abilities, recognize familiar Pokémon, and build a better understanding of how APIs power modern applications. Keep exploring, keep searching, and use this tool to sharpen both your curiosity and your development skills 🚀.
